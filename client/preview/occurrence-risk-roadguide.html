Occurrence Risk as the primary Road Guide signal

The accident occurrence model now drives the route risk number, route ranking, and the "safest route" recommendation. The multiclass severity model is demoted to secondary, per-segment detail. Same endpoint (/api/risk/route); all values degrade gracefully when occurrence or severity data is missing.

Fallback rules
• No occurrence on a route → that route falls back to its severity score only for ranking, and shows "N/A" occurrence.
• No occurrence on a segment → gauge shows "—"/N/A, severity still renders.
• No severity_probabilities → severity block hidden entirely.
• Backend offline / old response → screens behave exactly as today.

Requirement: "if occurrence or severity is missing, the app should not crash." Every binding is null-guarded and the missing block is hidden or shows a neutral placeholder.
