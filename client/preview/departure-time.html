Best time to leave

Compares the safest departure windows for the currently selected destination. Backed by POST /api/risk/route/departure-options with baseline_timestamp, window_hours, and step_minutes. Card shows up to 6 slots in a horizontally scrollable strip, the recommended slot is highlighted, and the reasoning line spells out the percentage delta vs. leave-now.

State
Interaction model:
• Card only appears once a destination is selected; before that the guidance sheet shows the existing search UI.
• Tap a slot → snaps as the active selection and re-runs requestRouteGuidance with the new timestamp so the route layer recolours to that future window.
• Tap Notify me → schedules a local Expo notification 10 min before the chosen time. Falls back gracefully if notification permission is denied.
• Tap Use this time → updates the active guidance preset to that future timestamp.
• "All similar" banner appears when the spread between the best and worst window is less than 5 percentage points — prevents the card from feeling like advice when there's no real difference.
